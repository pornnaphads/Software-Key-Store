# Product

## Register

product

## Users

SoftKeyStore serves Thai consumers, independent professionals, and small
businesses buying genuine digital software licenses. Users need to compare
products quickly, understand license terms, pay with familiar methods, receive
keys immediately, and recover confidently when activation or payment fails.

## Product Purpose

SoftKeyStore is a trustworthy digital software storefront for Windows,
Microsoft Office, creative tools, security software, and related licenses.
Success means users can discover the right license, verify price and coverage,
complete checkout without ambiguity, and retrieve their order or license key
after purchase.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, direct, and modern. The product should feel technically competent
without becoming a dark, neon, or decorative technology showcase. Important
commercial facts should be easier to see than promotional styling.

## Anti-references

- Dark neon gaming stores with excessive glow, glass, or animated decoration.
- Generic marketplace templates with inconsistent cards and crowded badges.
- Luxury styling that lowers contrast or hides important purchase information.
- Interfaces that use modal dialogs for ordinary filtering, editing, or help.
- Storefronts that obscure license scope, delivery timing, or refund conditions.

## Design Principles

1. Put purchase confidence before decoration.
2. Make license scope, stock, delivery, support, and totals immediately legible.
3. Use one consistent interaction vocabulary across all routes.
4. Keep common commerce patterns familiar and predictable.
5. Design every state, including loading, empty, invalid, unavailable, and
   successful outcomes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA across desktop, tablet, and mobile. Maintain readable
contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic form labels, useful error messages,
reduced-motion behavior, and pointer targets that meet minimum size or spacing
requirements. Thai and English content must reflow without clipping or
horizontal page scrolling.
